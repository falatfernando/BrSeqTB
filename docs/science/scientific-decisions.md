# BrSeqTB — Scientific Decision Register

This document tracks scientific questions and decisions that may affect the
behavior, interpretation, validation, or architecture of BrSeqTB.

A question must not be converted into a production implementation change
until the required evidence and scientific review have been completed.

---

## DEC-001 — Minimum Allele Frequency for Resistance-Associated Variants

**Status:** OPEN  
**Priority:** HIGH  
**Related assumption:** SA-003  
**Owner:** Scientific review required

### Question

Is the current 5% allele-frequency threshold unnecessarily restrictive for
detecting clinically/scientifically relevant low-frequency MTB
resistance-associated variants?

### Current evidence

Resistance-associated variants have been observed at AF values below 5%,
including approximately 1% AF in BDQ-associated observations and approximately
4% AF in another pipeline with phenotype-confirmed resistance.

### Important uncertainty

Lower AF thresholds may increase sensitivity but can also increase false
positives caused by sequencing error, alignment artifacts, low-complexity
regions, and insufficient read support.

### Required evidence

- phenotype-confirmed isolates;
- LoFreq quality metrics;
- alternate read counts;
- mapping/base quality;
- strand support;
- local sequence context;
- comparison with MAGMA;
- comparison with other validated MTB pipelines.

### Decision

OPEN

### Implementation change authorized?

NO

## DEC-004 — Apparent BDQ-Associated GATK Frameshift Calls

**Status:** OPEN  
**Priority:** CRITICAL  
**Related assumptions:** SA-011

### Question

What causes apparent bedaquiline resistance-associated frameshift/
heteroresistance calls produced by the current GATK workflow when other
pipelines exclude them and phenotype indicates susceptibility?

### Candidate explanations

- sequencing artifact;
- homopolymer error;
- alignment ambiguity;
- local assembly;
- indel representation;
- variant normalization;
- annotation;
- low-frequency sequencing noise;
- interaction between caller and downstream interpretation.

### Required investigation

Inspect:

- raw reads;
- BAM alignment;
- local sequence context;
- homopolymer structure;
- mapping quality;
- base quality;
- AF;
- alternate read support;
- strand support;
- GATK representation;
- normalized representation;
- annotation;
- LoFreq result;
- MAGMA result;
- phenotype.

### Decision

OPEN

### Implementation change authorized?

NO
